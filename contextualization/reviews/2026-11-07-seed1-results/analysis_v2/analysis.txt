## 2. Paired per-fact contrasts between arms (one Holm family per table)

Each table = one (contrast, cohort, pass, metric); its dose x label cells form the prespecified Holm family (`p_wilcoxon` raw, `p_holm` adjusted, `family_size` = tested cells). `p_wilcoxon` is the two-sided scipy Wilcoxon signed-rank p on the per-fact paired differences when every semantic group contributes one fact (`p_wilcoxon_level` = fact) and on the GROUP-level mean differences otherwise (level = group; finding 18, 2026-10-14) -- n/a below 10 non-zero values at that level; `p_wilcoxon_fact` (CSV only) is the naive per-fact p, given only when it coincides with the test used. `mean_abs_diff` = mean |paired difference| (per group where the cohort holds one record per group). Items: common items of every arm with the pass (finding 7). CIs: cluster bootstrap over semantic groups. The `all/all` row is descriptive and outside the family. No equivalence claim is made from any non-significant difference: without --equivalence-margin M no TOST is run, and 'not significant' is not 'equal'. Per-fact tests and bootstrap intervals describe within-run uncertainty only (facts sharing a training run are not independent training replicates). Uncertainty across training runs is estimated only from replicates and is unmeasured with one run per arm.

## 6. Uncertainty across training replicates

Per-fact tests and bootstrap intervals describe within-run uncertainty only (facts sharing a training run are not independent training replicates). Uncertainty across training runs is estimated only from replicates and is unmeasured with one run per arm.

Replicates found: 1 (rep0). Training-run variation is therefore UNMEASURED here; every interval and p-value in this report is within-run.

## 7. Equivalence

No equivalence claim is made from any non-significant difference: without --equivalence-margin M no TOST is run, and 'not significant' is not 'equal'.
